About me

From a young age, I’ve had a deep interest in the human body and overall well-being. In 2016, I completed my studies as a Physiotherapy Assistant and soon after began working in hotels in Crete, in the beautiful area of Agios Nikolaos. Working with people from all over the world opened up new horizons for me and inspired me to grow both personally and professionally. Today, I collaborate as an independent therapist with boutique hotels, villas, and Airbnb hosts, offering personalized massage sessions directly at the clients’ space. To me, massage is not just a service it’s an experience that should be fully tailored to each person’s unique needs and energy. My goal is to build a dedicated team of professionals who provide high quality care with respect for your time and individual requirements. If you’re looking for professionalism, quality and a warm human touch, I would be delighted to meet you in person.
